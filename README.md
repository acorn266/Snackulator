# 🍽️ Snackulator

> **Eat it. Log it. Know your numbers.**

Snackulator is a simple, privacy-focused nutrition tracker for keeping track of your daily food, calories, macros, and water — without accounts, subscriptions, or unnecessary complexity.

## ✨ Features

- 📊 **Daily Dashboard** — Track calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber & water.
- 🍎 **Food Logger** — Log meals and automatically calculate nutrition.
- 📚 **Personal Food Library** — Create, edit, search and manage your foods.
- 🎯 **Custom Targets** — Set your own daily nutrition goals.
- 📅 **History & Streaks** — Keep track of your progress over time.
- 💾 **Import & Export** — Back up and restore your nutrition data.
- 🔒 **Local-first** — Your data stays in your browser.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- HTML5
- CSS3

- Browser Local Storage

No backend. No accounts. Just open and use.
